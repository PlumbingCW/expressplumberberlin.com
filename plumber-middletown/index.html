Older buildings, hidden leaks

Middletown's older housing stock and downtown buildings often hide leaks behind plaster and in crawlspaces. A slow leak can rot framing for months before a stain shows. Our leak detection service uses meter and pressure tests and listening gear to find the source before walls are opened.

Rental properties and multi-family buildings add another layer, since the question of which line belongs to which unit matters. We describe the repair and the price to the owner or manager before starting.

Before We Arrive: If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak

In an older building, one failed fitting can soak several floors. Use these steps before the plumber gets there.

Shut off the main valve

Locate the building main or the unit shutoff and close it clockwise. In a multi-unit building, tell the manager at once.

Kill power to wet areas

Do not touch outlets or equipment in standing water. Cut the breaker for that area only if you can reach it dry.

Clear the area

Clear the floor below the leak, move electronics up and protect valuables. Photograph the damage.

Call the 24/7 line

Call the 24/7 line with the address and where the water is coming from. A licensed plumber answers.

City water and sewer in Middletown

The Middletown Water Department supplies the city's water, according to state records and city information. Ask them about your meter, your bill and any service line questions. For sewer concerns, check with the city about whether your home connects to the public system.

Problems with the main sewer lateral show up as slow drains in several fixtures. See sewer line cleaning, sewer camera inspection and sewer line repair.

Downtown restaurants and shops can call for after-hours help. See commercial plumbing for what we handle.

Student housing, rentals and older buildings

Middletown has a large rental market, including student housing near the university and older multi-family buildings downtown. These buildings share stacks and laterals, so a blockage can show up in a different unit than the one that caused it. We trace the shared line, tell the owner or manager what we found and keep the repair clear of other tenants' spaces.

Turnover between lease terms is a good time for planned work such as water heater installation, and we coordinate scheduling so a unit is not out of hot water longer than necessary.

Codes and licensing
