Before We Arrive: If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak

These steps limit damage while a plumber is on the way. They take a few minutes and they matter more than anything else you can do.

Shut off the main valve

Find the main valve where the water line enters the house, usually in the basement, and turn it clockwise until it stops.

Kill power to wet areas

If water reaches outlets or the panel, do not touch them. Switch off the breaker only if you can reach it without standing in water.

Clear the area

Move rugs, boxes and electronics out of the wet area. Lift items off the floor and photograph the damage for your insurer.

Call the 24/7 line

Tell the dispatcher your street, what is leaking and whether you have shut off the main. A licensed plumber will answer.
