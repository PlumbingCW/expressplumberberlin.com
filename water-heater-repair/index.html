When a water heater failure is an emergency

A water heater that leaks onto the floor or smells of gas needs fast action. The 24/7 line is open, and a licensed plumber will tell you what to shut off while a crew is arranged. Close the cold water valve on top of the tank, and leave the building before calling if you smell gas.

Most water heater calls begin with one of four symptoms: no hot water at all, hot water that runs out too fast, water that is lukewarm, or water around the base of the tank. Each points somewhere different. No hot water on an electric unit often means a tripped breaker, a failed element or a bad thermostat. On a gas unit, the first suspects are the pilot assembly, thermocouple and gas control valve.

Popping or rumbling is sediment cooking at the bottom of the tank. Rusty hot water, with clear cold water, suggests the inside of the tank or the anode rod is wearing out. A puddle is the one symptom to act on immediately, because the source decides everything. A loose fitting or a failed relief valve is a repair. A seam leak in the tank shell is the end of the tank.

Safe checks before you call

  • Electric unit: look for a tripped breaker, but do not open the access panels yourself.
  • Gas unit: if you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 before calling us.
  • Leak: close the cold water supply valve on top of the tank if you can reach it safely.
  • Note the unit age from the data plate, usually on the side of the tank.
  • Keep children and pets away from hot water piping and standing water near electrical outlets.

Repair or replace: how we decide

A repair makes sense when the tank is sound and the failed part is inexpensive compared with the unit. Thermostats, elements, thermocouples, relief valves and drain valves fall in that group. A tank that leaks through its steel shell cannot be repaired, and a unit that has needed several parts in a short time is telling you it is near the end.

Age matters, but not as the only fact. Tank water heaters often serve roughly ten to fifteen years, depending on water quality, maintenance and how hard the unit works. We read the data plate, look at the anode condition if we can, and tell you straight when money spent on repair will not buy much more life. If replacement is the better path, we move into water heater replacement and set a clear price before any work starts.

Repair usually fits when

  • Tank is sound and dry.
  • One part has failed.
  • Unit is within its expected life.
  • The part cost is small next to a new unit.

Replacement usually fits when

  • Water seeps from the tank shell.
  • Several parts failed in a short time.
  • Rust shows in hot water.
  • The unit is well past its expected life.

Gas, electric and hybrid units in Berlin homes

Berlin homes run a mix of fuels. Gas units need combustion air, a safe vent and a gas connection that passes a leak test after any work. Electric units need a correct breaker and wiring for their element load. Hybrid or heat pump units add a compressor, a condensate drain and clearance for airflow, and they behave differently in a cold basement.

Hard water shortens the life of every type. We do not quote a hardness figure for Berlin because the town is served by more than one water system, so the number depends on your street. Ask your water utility for its annual water quality report, or test your tap, and we will adjust our flushing advice to match.

Maintenance that prevents the next call

Flushing the tank clears sediment that insulates the burner or element from the water. Checking the temperature and pressure relief valve confirms it is not stuck. Inspecting the expansion tank on closed systems prevents repeated pressure spikes that stress fittings.

If you are weighing a different type, our tankless water heater page covers flushing, descaling and what changes in the installation. For a planned swap with no emergency pressure, see water heater installation.

Codes and licensing

Connecticut's State Building Code, effective October 1, 2022, adopts the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC). Relief valves, discharge lines and connections on water heating equipment follow that code, and fuel, venting and burner work can fall under other licensed trades. Permits and inspections in Berlin are handled by the local building office, and we confirm what a job needs before it starts.
