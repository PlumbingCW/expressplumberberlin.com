How a tankless heater differs from a tank

A tankless unit fires a burner or element only when water flows, so there is no stored tank to run out. The trade-off is a high demand for gas or electric capacity and tight tolerances in flow, venting and water quality. If any of those drift, the unit shows an error code or delivers uneven temperatures.

Many Berlin homes were built around tank heaters, so a tankless upgrade often means a larger gas line, new venting and a condensate drain. A good installation starts with a load check, not just a unit swap.

Flush and descale: the maintenance most units miss

Mineral scale builds on the heat exchanger and narrows its passages. Symptoms include fluctuating temperature, longer wait for heat, error codes and noise. A scheduled flush with a circulation pump and descaling solution removes scale before it damages the exchanger.

How fast scale builds depends on your water. Berlin is served by several public water systems, and state records list the Berlin Water Control Commission, Kensington Fire District and Worthington Fire District among them. We do not quote hardness for your street, so test your tap or read your water utility's annual report, then we set the flush interval to match.

Signs a tankless unit needs service

  • Temperature swings during a shower.
  • An error code that returns after resetting.
  • Longer delay before hot water arrives.
  • Rumbling or ticking from the unit.
  • Reduced flow at fixtures even though the supply is fine.

The isolation valves on a tankless installation matter more than most owners realize. Without them, flushing is difficult and often skipped. We install service valves on every new tankless job so maintenance is possible.

Repair, replacement or a different approach

Not every tankless problem is the heater. A clogged inlet screen, a worn flow sensor or a kinked vent can mimic a failed unit. We check the cheap and common causes first and tell you what we found before recommending any replacement.

If a tankless unit is past repair, we compare a new tankless unit with a tank on the real numbers for your home. For emergency situations with no hot water, start with water heater repair. For a planned tank swap, see water heater installation.

Error codes and what they usually mean

Every maker has a code list, and the meanings vary. In general, codes point to a few areas: ignition problems, flow problems, venting problems, temperature sensors or scale. Writing down the code and what you were doing when it appeared helps a plumber narrow the cause before arriving.

A reset sometimes clears a one-time fault. If the code returns, it is telling you something real. Do not keep resetting a unit with a recurring gas or vent code, and call for service.

Cold water sandwich and other quirks

A short burst of cold water between two hot ones, often called the cold water sandwich, can happen when you turn the tap off and on quickly. The unit shuts down and has to reheat. A small buffer tank or a recirculation setup reduces it. It is not a defect, but it is something to know before you buy.

Minimum flow is another quirk. Very low flow may not trigger the burner, so a barely open tap can stay cool. Aerators with tiny openings, or scale, can also trigger it. We test flow at several fixtures after an installation.

Codes and licensing

Connecticut's State Building Code, effective October 1, 2022, adopts the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC). Relief valves, discharge lines and connections on water heating equipment follow that code, and fuel, venting and burner work can fall under other licensed trades. Permits and inspections in Berlin are handled by the local building office, and we confirm what a job needs before it starts.
