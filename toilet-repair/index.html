When a toilet problem is an emergency

A toilet that overflows, leaks at the base or will not stop filling is an emergency, because water reaches the floor quickly. Close the shutoff valve behind the toilet by turning it clockwise, lift the tank lid and hold the flapper closed if the bowl is rising, then call the 24/7 line. A licensed plumber will tell you what to do while a crew is arranged.

If the toilet clogs with other fixtures slow or gurgling, the blockage may be deeper than the bowl. Stop using water in the house until we know. For a plain bowl clog, a plunger designed for toilets often works, and we tell you when to stop trying.

First steps for an overflowing toilet

  • Turn the shutoff valve behind the toilet clockwise.
  • Lift the tank lid and hold the flapper down if water keeps rising.
  • Do not flush again.
  • Move rugs and towels away from the base.
  • Stop other water use if bathtubs or sinks gurgle.

Repair or replace the toilet

Most toilet problems are repair jobs. Flappers, fill valves, wax rings and bolts are small parts. We replace the toilet when the bowl is cracked, the glaze is crazed, the flush is poor even after cleaning or the toilet is an older high-use fixture that wastes water.

If you choose a new toilet, we help with sizing, rough-in distance and seat height. We set it on a new seal, test it for leaks and haul away the old one. Older homes in Kensington and East Berlin sometimes have flanges that no longer sit flush with the floor, so we check them as part of the job.

Water use and Berlin households

Toilets are among the biggest users of water in a home. A running toilet can add to your bill without any visible damage, which is why a stuck flapper is worth fixing quickly. Berlin is served by several public water systems, so check with your own water supplier for how your bill is calculated.

If you are weighing a high-efficiency toilet, ask us about flush performance and parts. A good fixture clears the bowl in one flush and is easy to repair.

Choosing a toilet that stays out of trouble

A good toilet clears waste in one flush, has easy-to-find parts and does not run. When choosing, ask about the flush mechanism and the fill valve brand. A common, replaceable fill valve means a cheap repair years later.

Comfort height, elongated bowls and wall-hung models are popular, and each has trade-offs for installation. Wall-hung models need a carrier inside the wall. We tell you what the job involves before you buy.
