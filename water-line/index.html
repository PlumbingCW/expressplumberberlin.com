When a water line break is an emergency

A break in the service line can drop your pressure to nothing or flood part of the yard. If water bubbles up in the lawn or the driveway, shut off the main valve inside the house and call the 24/7 line. A licensed plumber will tell you what to do while a crew is arranged.

If you have no water at all, check whether neighbors are affected. Your water supplier handles main breaks in the street, and your plumber handles the line on your property. We can help sort out which one you have.

First steps

  • Shut off the main valve inside the house.
  • Check whether neighbors lost water too.
  • Note wet or sinking ground in the yard.
  • Do not dig near the line without a utility mark-out.
  • Call your water supplier if the problem appears to be in the street.

Symptoms that point to the service line

A service line leak can look like a high bill, a wet patch in the yard, a loud hiss near the meter or pressure that falls all at once. Cold, wet ground in winter or a green patch in dry weather can also point to a leak. Frozen ground can break pipes that are too shallow.

Repair or replace the line

A short repair works when the damage is a single failed joint on a line that is otherwise sound. A line that has corroded or been repaired several times is a better candidate for replacement. Older lines may be galvanized steel, copper or early plastic, and the material tells us a lot about how long it will last.

Trenchless replacement can reduce digging in some cases, with entry pits at each end. Open trench replacement is simpler on a short run and a clear path. We explain what the route looks like, where utilities cross and how the yard will be restored.

Who is responsible for which part in Berlin

Berlin is served by more than one public water system, according to state records. These include the Berlin Water Control Commission, Kensington Fire District and Worthington Fire District, and nearby systems such as Cromwell Fire District Water also serve parts of town. Your supplier owns the main in the street and usually the meter. You usually own the line from the meter or curb stop to your house.

Ask your supplier where the responsibility changes on your street. Call before you dig. Connecticut's Call Before You Dig system marks underground utilities for free, and we handle that notice for any excavation we do.

What a replacement visit involves

We locate the line, call for utility mark-out and decide the route. We shut off the water, expose the pipe at the house and at the meter or curb stop, and replace the line between them. We make connections with the right fittings, pressure test and flush the line before it goes back in service.

Afterward we backfill, compact and tell you what restoration remains. If the line runs under a drive or a walk, we discuss the options and the added cost.

Codes and licensing

Connecticut's State Building Code, effective October 1, 2022, adopts the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC). Supply piping, pressure and material rules for this work follow that code. Permits and inspections in Berlin are handled by the local building office, and we confirm what a job needs before it starts.
