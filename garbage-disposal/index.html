When a disposal problem is an emergency

A disposal that leaks water under the cabinet or smokes is an emergency. Turn off the switch and the breaker, and call the 24/7 line. A licensed plumber will tell you what to do while a crew is arranged. Never put your hand in the disposal, even when it is off.

A jam or hum with no leak is less urgent but still stops dishes and cooking. Many jams clear with a hex wrench turned in the base, a reset button and patience. We tell you how to try it safely on the phone.

Safe steps for a jammed disposal

  • Switch the disposal off and cut power at the breaker.
  • Never reach into the unit with a hand.
  • Use the hex socket under the unit to turn the motor shaft by hand.
  • Press the red reset button on the base.
  • Call us if it still hums or the sink holds water.

Choosing and installing a new disposal

Disposals vary by motor strength, grind stage and noise insulation. A household that cooks often benefits from a stronger motor. A small kitchen with a dishwasher may only need a basic unit. We match the model to the sink, the drain and the household.

Installation includes the mounting ring, the dishwasher connection if there is one, the drain connection and an electrical check. We confirm the power supply and the plug or hard wire. We then test for leaks with water running and the unit on.

Care that extends disposal life

Run cold water while the disposal is on and for a short time after. Avoid grease, coffee grounds in large amounts, fibrous peels, bones and starchy foods that swell, such as rice and pasta. Septic systems add another reason to be careful, so ask the town or a septic professional if your home uses one.

Berlin kitchens with older drain lines are more likely to clog from grease, and a disposal does not change that. If your kitchen sink drains slowly, see drain cleaning or ask about hydro jetting for repeat grease buildup.

Noise, smell and performance problems

A disposal that sounds like gravel is grinding something hard, such as a bone or a piece of glass. A rattle with no grind may be a spoon that fell in. A smell is almost always old food in the chamber or the drain beyond it. Grinding ice and citrus peel, then flushing with cold water, freshens it for a while.

If the smell comes from the drain and not the chamber, the trap or the line has buildup. Our drain cleaning service clears it, and a regular habit of flushing with plenty of water helps keep it clear.
