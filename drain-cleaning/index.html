What to do first when a drain backs up

Berlin homeowners usually notice a clog at the worst time: a washing machine drains and the shower floor starts to fill. Stop using water in the house and keep laundry and dishwashers off. Every gallon you add has to go somewhere, and a stopped line sends it to the lowest opening.

We cover drain emergencies at night, on weekends and on holidays. Tell the dispatcher which fixtures are involved, because that tells us whether the clog sits in one branch or in the main line. If only the kitchen sink is slow, the problem is almost always local to that branch. If the tub gurgles when the toilet flushes, the blockage is deeper.

While you wait for the plumber

  • Stop running sinks, showers, dishwashers and washing machines.
  • Do not pour chemical drain cleaner into standing water, it can splash and burn skin and it complicates the repair.
  • Move rugs and stored boxes away from the lowest floor drain.
  • Note which fixtures are slow or gurgling and when it started.
  • Know where your main water shutoff is in case a pipe is leaking, not just clogged.

How we find where the clog sits

A fixture-level clog and a main-line clog need different tools. For sinks, tubs and showers we use hand augers and small cable machines that reach into the trap and the first stretch of branch line. For floor drains, laundry standpipes and lines that run through the slab or crawlspace, a larger drum machine with cutting heads gives more reach.

When the cable meets resistance a long way from the fixture, the clog may be roots, a belly in the pipe or a broken joint. That is the point where a sewer camera inspection earns its cost, because it shows what the cable is hitting instead of guessing. If the camera shows grease buildup or scale, hydro jetting scours the pipe wall and keeps the line open longer than a cable alone.

Causes we see in older Central Connecticut homes

Berlin was incorporated in 1785, and Kensington and East Berlin hold housing from many different decades. That mix matters for drains. Older cast iron lines scale on the inside and catch paper and grease. Newer plastic lines are smoother but can hold a low spot where debris settles.

Kitchen lines carry the most avoidable trouble. Cooking fat cools in the pipe and builds a waxy layer that narrows the bore a little more each month. Bathroom clogs are usually hair and soap binding together near the trap. Laundry lines clog with lint, and a washer that suddenly overflows its standpipe is a classic sign. We tell you what we pulled out, because that points to what to change at the fixture.

If a clog clears and comes back, ask for a camera look before paying for repeat cabling. Repeated cabling of a broken or root-filled line treats the symptom and leaves the cause in place.

When a drain problem is really a sewer problem

One slow drain is a fixture issue. Several drains that slow down together, a toilet that burbles when the washer runs, or sewage smell near a floor drain all point to the main line. In that case the fix runs through the cleanout, which is usually a capped pipe in the basement, near the foundation wall or in the yard.

Main-line work moves into sewer line repair territory when the camera shows a crack, an offset joint or roots entering at a connection. We explain what the screen shows, whether a cleaning will hold, and what a repair would involve. You approve a clear price before any repair starts.
