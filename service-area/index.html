Where we work

Our home territory is Berlin, including Kensington and East Berlin. ZIP 06037 covers Berlin and Kensington and ZIP 06023 covers East Berlin. We also serve Newington, Cromwell, Southington, Middletown and New Britain, and each has a page with local detail.

We also reach Plainville, Plantsville and Milldale, small places that are mentioned on the Southington page. If your town is near ours and not listed, call. We will tell you honestly if the trip makes sense.

Distances matter in an emergency, so we ask for your street and the problem when you call. A licensed plumber then explains the plan.

A closer look at each town

Newington lies north of Berlin along Route 9 and the Berlin Turnpike. Its water and sewer are provided through the Metropolitan District, and its ZIP codes are 06111 and 06131. Calls there are mostly drains, water heaters and condo plumbing. See our Newington page.

Cromwell is a Middlesex County town southeast of Berlin, linked by Route 9. It has a single ZIP code, 06416, and is served by Cromwell Fire District Water, which also serves part of Berlin. See our Cromwell page for burst pipe and sewer line calls.

Southington is the large town west of Berlin, with its own water department, the village of Plantsville at ZIP 06479 and the main ZIP 06489. Basement water and water heaters dominate calls. See Southington.

Middletown, a Middlesex County city at the south end of Route 9, uses ZIPs 06457 and 06459 and has its own water department. Older buildings and rentals make leak detection a frequent request. See Middletown.

New Britain borders Berlin to the north and uses ZIPs 06051, 06052 and 06053. Dense housing with shared lines shapes the calls. See New Britain.

How coverage works in practice

Every town on this page has a dedicated page because the calls there are different. A plumber who works in Middletown's older downtown buildings needs a different plan from one in a Southington ranch house with a long sewer lateral. We write each page around what is true for that town.

We do not claim response times, because traffic, weather and the number of calls change them. What we do promise is an answer on the 24/7 line and a plain description of what happens next.

What to do when you call from a nearby town

  • Give the street address and the nearest cross street.
  • Tell us what is leaking, backing up or not working.
  • Say whether you have shut off water, power or gas.
  • Tell us if anyone is in danger or if water is near electrical equipment.
  • Let us know about gate codes, pets or parking.

If your town is not listed, call anyway. We will tell you honestly if the trip makes sense.

Codes and licensing

Connecticut's State Building Code, effective October 1, 2022, adopts the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC). Drain, waste, vent and fixture rules for work like this follow the plumbing provisions of that code. Permits and inspections in Berlin are handled by the local building office, and we confirm what a job needs before it starts.

  • Connecticut Department of Administrative Services, 2022 building and fire codes.
  • Connecticut Department of Consumer Protection, verify a plumbing license.
