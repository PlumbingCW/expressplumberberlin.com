What to do when plumbing fails in Berlin

A plumbing emergency has a clear first move. Shut off the water, keep people away from wet electrical areas and call the 24/7 line. A licensed plumber answers, asks what is happening and tells you what to do while a crew is arranged. Same-day appointments are offered when available.

Berlin is a Hartford County town of Kensington, East Berlin and the Berlin Turnpike corridor, and our shop sits on Farmington Avenue in Kensington. That makes the calls familiar. We know the older streets near the center, the newer subdivisions and the businesses along US Route 5, and we plan the visit around the building and the problem.

First steps in almost any plumbing emergency

  • Close the main water shutoff valve, usually where the line enters the house.
  • Stop using sinks, tubs, toilets and washers if drains are backing up.
  • Keep people and pets away from standing water near outlets.
  • Move belongings off the floor and photograph damage for your insurer.
  • Call the 24/7 line and describe what you see.

Emergencies we handle most

Burst and frozen pipes

Winter splits pipes in exterior walls and unheated spaces. We stop the water, repair the break and look for others. See burst pipe repair.

Backed-up drains and sewers

A slow main line becomes an overflow quickly. We clear it from the cleanout and check the pipe. See drain cleaning.

Failed water heaters

A leaking tank cannot be patched. We replace it or repair a sound one. See water heater replacement.

Hidden leaks

A jump in the water bill or a damp wall needs testing, not guessing. See leak detection.

Berlin, Kensington and East Berlin: how local details shape plumbing

Berlin was incorporated in 1785, and its housing reflects many eras. Homes from the older decades, including those near Kensington's center and the Worthington Meetinghouse, are the ones most likely to have cast iron drains and early supply piping. Newer homes bring their own calls, such as water heaters, sump pumps and fixtures. We never assume, because pipe age is a fact we confirm on site.

Berlin has Silver Lake, the Hanging Hills and the Metacomet and Mattabesett trails, and a lot of residents spend weekends outdoors and come home to a wet-boot basement entry. If your basement takes on water after storms, our sump pump service explains what to check first.

Route 9 bypasses Kensington to the north and east, and the Berlin station sits near the center. Businesses along the Berlin Turnpike, US Route 5, run kitchens, restrooms and shops that need after-hours help. See commercial plumbing.

Berlin is served by more than one public water system, according to Connecticut Department of Public Health records, including the Berlin Water Control Commission, Kensington Fire District and Worthington Fire District. We do not quote water hardness for the town, because it depends on your street. Test your tap or ask your water utility for its annual report.
