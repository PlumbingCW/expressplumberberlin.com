Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we collect when you call us or visit this site, and how we use it.

What we collect

This site does not have forms, and we do not ask you to enter personal details here. When you call, we may collect your phone number, your name, your address and a description of the problem so we can send a plumber.

Calls may be recorded for quality and training. We tell you at the start of the call. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the person who answers.

How we use it

We use call information to schedule and complete service, to follow up about the job and to improve how we answer calls. We do not sell personal information.

We may share information with a call-tracking or telephone service provider that handles our phone line, and with others as the law requires.

Website data

Our web host and the fonts and maps loaded on this site may collect technical data such as your IP address and browser type. Map and font providers have their own privacy policies.

We do not use the site to track you across other websites.

Your choices and contact

You can ask us to delete information we hold about you, subject to legal and business records requirements. Email contact@expressplumberberlin.com or call (203) 720-6269.
