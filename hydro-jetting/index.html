Sewer backup: what to do first

A main line that backs up into a floor drain or tub is an emergency. Water from every sink, shower and washer is trying to leave the house through one blocked pipe. Stop using water, keep laundry and dishwashers off, and call the 24/7 line so a licensed plumber can tell you what is safe to do while you wait.

Keep children and pets out of the affected area, because backed-up drain water carries bacteria. Do not use chemical cleaners in standing water. If finished space is wet, photograph it for your insurer before cleanup starts.

While you wait for the plumber

  • Stop using sinks, showers, toilets, dishwashers and washers.
  • Keep people and pets out of wet areas.
  • Do not pour drain chemicals into a backed-up line.
  • Locate your cleanout if you can do so safely.
  • Photograph any water damage for insurance.

Symptoms that point to jetting instead of cabling

Hydro jetting sends water through a hose and nozzle at pressure, scouring the whole circumference of the pipe. A cable cuts a channel through the middle of a blockage and leaves the coating on the walls. That is why a drain can run for a few weeks after cabling and then clog again.

Signs that jetting may fit include a line that clogs every few months, a kitchen line coated with grease, slow drains in several fixtures at once, and a camera view that shows scale or roots along the wall. If a broken pipe or an offset joint is causing the problem, jetting will not cure it, and we say so before any work begins.

How a jetting visit works

  1. Camera look first

    When the line allows, we view the pipe so we do not blast water at a break.

  2. Access through the cleanout

    The hose enters at your cleanout or another access point.

  3. Staged cleaning

    We start with a lower pressure pass and work up as the pipe allows.

  4. Verify flow

    We run water through fixtures and, where it helps, look again with the camera.

Jetting makes a mess only when the line is open to a floor drain, so we protect the area and tell you what to expect. After the job, we describe what came out and whether a repeat visit should be scheduled.

Older Berlin homes and why lines scale up

Berlin dates to 1785, and Kensington and East Berlin include homes of many ages. Older cast iron and clay lines roughen with age, so paper and grease catch more easily. Newer plastic lines are smoother, but a sag can hold water and debris.

Restaurants and shops along the Berlin Turnpike face a faster grease cycle than a typical house. Kitchens often schedule jetting as maintenance. For cleaning that does not need the full pressure treatment, see drain cleaning and sewer line cleaning.
