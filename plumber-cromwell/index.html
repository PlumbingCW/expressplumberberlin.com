Before We Arrive: If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak

Cold snaps turn small cracks into floods. These four steps keep a Cromwell basement dry while help is on the way.

Shut off the main valve

Turn the main shutoff clockwise where the water line enters the house. If it is stuck, tell the dispatcher right away.

Kill power to wet areas

Do not stand in water near outlets. Cut the breaker for the wet area only if the panel is dry and reachable.

Clear the area

Pull belongings off the floor, open cabinet doors under sinks and photograph what you see for your insurer.
