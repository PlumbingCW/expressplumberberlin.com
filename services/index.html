How to choose the right service

Start with the problem. If water is spreading, go to burst pipe repair and close the main valve. If drains are slow, see drain cleaning. If you are out of hot water, see water heater repair. Each page explains what to check, what we do and what affects the price.

Planned jobs have their own pages. Water heater installation, repiping and plumbing installation are scheduled work, and we plan them with you. Businesses can start with commercial plumbing.

Every page names real causes and honest options. We give a clear price before work starts, and we tell you when a repair is not worth it.

Emergency work and planned work

Some pages describe emergencies, such as burst pipes, sewer backups and failed water heaters. Others describe planned work, such as installations, repiping and waterproofing. The pages are written differently on purpose. An emergency page tells you what to do in the first minutes. A planned page tells you what to decide and what to prepare.

If you are not sure which page applies, call. A licensed plumber will point you to the right fix.

Codes and licensing

Connecticut's State Building Code, effective October 1, 2022, adopts the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC). Drain, waste, vent and fixture rules for work like this follow the plumbing provisions of that code. Permits and inspections in Berlin are handled by the local building office, and we confirm what a job needs before it starts.

  • Connecticut Department of Administrative Services, 2022 building and fire codes.
  • Connecticut Department of Consumer Protection, verify a plumbing license.
