Before We Arrive: If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak

In a multi-family building, a few minutes of action protect the neighbors as well. Run through these steps now.

Shut off the main valve

Close the unit shutoff or the building main clockwise. Tell the landlord or manager if the water is spreading to other units.

Kill power to wet areas

Do not touch wet outlets. Turn off the breaker for the area only if you can reach it safely and dry.

Clear the area

Move belongings out of the wet area, put towels at doorways and photograph the damage.

Call the 24/7 line

Call the 24/7 line with the address and unit. A licensed plumber answers and guides you.

Two-family houses and shared lines

New Britain has many two- and three-family houses, and in those buildings the plumbing is shared by design. A single water heater may serve two units, one main shutoff may control the whole house and one drain stack may carry every unit. Before we start, we ask who lives where and which fixtures are affected, so we do not shut off water to a neighbor without notice.

We tell the owner what we found and what the repair requires, and we leave a short note of what was done.
