We take jobs by phone so a plumber can ask the right questions and tell you what to do while you wait. We do not use an online request form.

What to tell us when you call

Have this ready

  • Your street address and the nearest cross street.
  • What is happening, in your own words.
  • Whether you have shut off the water or power.
  • Whether anyone is in danger or water is near electrical equipment.
  • The age and fuel of the water heater if that is the problem.

Calls may be recorded for quality and training, and we say so at the start of the call.

Hours

The emergency line is open 24 hours a day, every day, including holidays. Planned jobs such as installations are scheduled during regular working hours, and we confirm the time when you call.
